        📖 Linkup User Manual

        📻 Real-time Live Talk (Live PTT)

        Unlike standard audio files, Live Talk Mode broadcasts live audio straight through your friend's speakers in real time!

        • How to Talk: Tap the 📻 Radio PTT button in the top right of any chat to open the dial.
        • Press & Hold: Hold the large circular PTT TALK button (or hold the [Spacebar] key) while speaking.
        • Radio Sound Effects: You'll hear realistic radio squelch static when opening the frequency, and a Roger Beep when you release the button.
        • Startup Voice: Every time you open the app, your radio dispatcher announces that your frequency is active and standing by!

        🎙️ Voice Notes (Recorded Audio Messages)

        Voice notes are recorded audio messages saved directly into the chat timeline so your contact can play them back whenever convenient.

        • Recording: Press and hold the 🎙️ mic button on the bottom right of the input bar.
        • Cancel Recording: While holding, slide your finger or mouse to the left to cancel without sending.
        • Playback: Tap the ▶ Play button on any voice note bubble to listen with animated wave bars.

        💬 Adding Friends & Sharing Media

        Exchange messages, photos, camera snaps, and documents directly peer-to-peer.

        • Add Contact: Click ➕ in the header and enter a user's Linkup ID (e.g. ECHO00 to test).
        • Send Files & Photos: Tap the 📎 paperclip to attach documents, photos, or use your camera. You can also paste or drag and drop files directly.
        • Ticks: 🕒 = Sending, ✓ = Sent to network, ✓✓ = Delivered, ✓✓ = Read.

        📞 HD Voice & Video Calls

        Direct peer-to-peer audio and video calls with encrypted WebRTC streams.

        • Start Call: Tap 📞 for voice call or 📹 for video call in the top chat header.
        • Controls: Toggle camera on/off, mute microphone, or minimize call to picture-in-picture.

        ⚙️ Customization, Themes & Logout

        Customize your profile, personalize your theme, or switch users.

        • Themes: Open Settings ⚙️ to switch between ☀️ Light, 🌙 Dark, or 🖥️ System theme.
        • Custom ID: Set a personalized, memorable Linkup ID anytime in Settings.
        • Sign Out: Use the Log Out button in Settings to safely disconnect and log in with another account.
