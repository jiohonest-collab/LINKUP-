                          📖 Linkup User Manual

                          💬 WhatsApp-Style Messaging

                          Full realtime messaging with delivery ticks (🕒 Pending, ✓ Sent, ✓✓ Delivered, ✓✓ Read).

                          • Reactions: Hover over any message to select an emoji reaction (👍, ❤️, 😂, 😮, 😢, 🙏).
                          • Quoted Replies: Click the reply icon on a bubble to quote it in your response.
                          • Formatting: Use *bold*, _italic_, ~strikethrough~, or `code`.
                          • Attachments: Send documents, photos, polls, live webcam snaps, or GPS location pins.
                          • Search in Chat: Use 🔍 in the header to search through conversation history.

                          📻 Live Push-to-Talk (Walkie-Talkie Radio)

                          Broadcasts live audio directly through peer speakers with authentic radio squelch bursts and Roger Beeps!

                          • Open Radio Drawer: Click the 📻 Radio PTT button in the top right of any chat.
                          • Speak Live: Press and hold the big PTT TALK button (or hold the [Spacebar] on desktop).
                          • Channel Tuner: Switch between Channels 1-16 to communicate on private group frequencies.

                          🎙️ Voice Notes (Recorded Audio Memos)

                          Send recorded audio messages that are saved into the chat timeline.

                          • Record: Press and hold the 🎙️ mic button in the bottom input bar.
                          • Cancel: Slide your finger or mouse to the left while holding to cancel without sending.
                          • Playback: Tap Play (▶) on any voice memo to listen with interactive waveform and 1x/1.5x/2x speed selector.

                          ⭕ 24-Hour Status Updates

                          Post photo or text updates that automatically expire after 24 hours.

                          • Post Status: Switch to the Status tab and tap ✏️ Text Status or 📷 Photo Status.
                          • View Friends' Statuses: Tap on any contact's status ring to launch the fullscreen viewer with progress bars.
                          • Who Viewed: Tap the eye (👁️) icon on your status to see who viewed your update.

                          📞 Encrypted Audio & Video Calls

                          Peer-to-peer WebRTC calls with Picture-in-Picture mode.

                          • Start Call: Tap 📞 for Voice Call or 📹 for Video Call in the conversation header.
                          • Controls: Flip camera, mute microphone, or minimize call to picture-in-picture while chatting.

                          ⚙️ Custom Themes & ID Settings

                          Personalize your app appearance and manage multiple accounts.

                          • Themes: Switch between 🌙 Dark, 🖤 AMOLED Black, 🌑 Charcoal, or ☀️ Light.
                          • Custom ID: Choose a personalized 4-20 character Linkup ID in Settings.
